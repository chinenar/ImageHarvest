# OCR 1.6.76

This revision fixes small Japanese labels on the mixed-language plant page and removes an unused placement analysis pass. It does not solve automatic sound-effect classification or demonstrate faster whole-page OCR.

## Recognition and ownership

- Infer small-region script from two different, high-confidence CJK labels agreeing at the same positions across two prepared inputs. A damaged neighboring line no longer vetoes this evidence. One label, different positions, or reuse of identical inference is insufficient.
- For these small CJK reads, retain a padded-crop line as a neighboring observation instead of promoting it when another detector region owns over 80% of the line and the requested region owns less than half. Source pixels and raw observations remain available. Ordinary edge overhang is retained.
- Normalize compatibility forms for voting, including fullwidth punctuation. Preserve the selected reading's spelling. A one-glyph CJK fragment can be recovered from two agreeing, high-confidence longer readings at the same position.
- Keep a bounded 64-entry cache of recognition text/geometry keyed by exact prepared pixels, language, engine, device, recognition mode and coordinate transform. No image buffers or tensors are retained. Clear on GPU fallback. Identical inference counts as one vote, with provenance exposed in the result.

The previous eight-case full-image regression run retained 11/11 unique lexical matches, with 10/11 exact punctuation matches and no detections in the annotated negative areas. The short heading still has two dots instead of three. General tests passed 177/177, and the OCR suite passed 82/82. The packaged ASAR matched all 122 application source files.

## Performance boundaries

Without text refinement, placement previously calculated split hints and then discarded the entire preliminary result before computing the final, competition-aware layout. It now runs only the final pass. Refinement keeps its original sequence because the two modes produce different geometry.

On 18 existing regions from one real page, four alternating warm pairs measured 768–823 ms before versus 389–407 ms after. All final placement records were identical. This measures placement only, not OCR or translation-provider time. Reproduce with `scripts/check-ocr-layout-speed.cjs`.

Cache A/B on the full plant page saved only 2 of 262 inferences. Enabled took 24.749 s and disabled 21.494 s; recognition phases were essentially equal (9.811 vs 9.799 s). A noisy, unscored region varied between these runs. Therefore no full-page speedup or complete equality claim is made. The final run after label fixes took 23.884 s in this environment; it is a single observation, not an optimization claim.

## Remaining work

Automatic sound-effect classification, missing text elsewhere on the page, unknown-script short labels, and robust speed across the full corpus remain unresolved. Do not infer success from execution completion or extrapolate these regional results to all 589 images.
