# OCR 1.6.87

Batch of evidence-gated fixes for rotated Latin margin text, ambiguous short labels, small edge text, terminal periods and partially recognized ellipses. Full-page recognition recovers POISON, stop...STOP, the rotated margin sentence, my body., difficult., and G-GOOD-FOR-N... without target words in production rules.

New modules keep routing/evidence, pixel punctuation analysis, and overlapping-reading review separate. Rotation is recorded in reproducible crop metadata with inverse coordinate mapping. Small recrops require agreement on distinct image inputs plus support from the existing English reading or the multilingual model; candidates that add unrelated nearby text or change line layout are rejected. Period correction requires a round baseline component under two thresholds plus a same-position reading, or two agreeing tight recrops. A high-confidence two-dot reading can recover a third dot only from three source components.

Unresolved handwriting is handled explicitly: a sparse glyph read as 5 gets the alternative ち from Manga OCR, retained for review rather than promoted from generative self-agreement. No broad manga-model reading of artwork is accepted. At most two sparse proposals per page receive these probes. Overlapping independent recognition lines retain both words and are excluded from default automatic translation pending review. User edits and explicit confirmation invalidate the corresponding review signatures. This is a review guard, not automatic pixel ownership or separation of the touching POISON/Water Edge lettering. The longer handwritten sound effect across artwork remains unresolved.

Validation: 199 general tests pass; 118 desktop JavaScript files pass syntax. Eight development cases on six source images retain 11/11 lexical references and improve punctuation-inclusive single-match references from 10/11 to 11/11. The plant page has 12/12 correct best-match transcriptions including punctuation (whitespace ignored), while its strict single-match count remains 11/12 due to the overlapping label. Five plant target sentences and the heading are separately checked including normalized spaces. Romance-page region text remains identical to 1.6.86. OCR Lab interaction tests verify selecting the kana alternative, saving review status, and showing overlap warnings. These are development regressions, not whole-corpus accuracy or speed certification.
